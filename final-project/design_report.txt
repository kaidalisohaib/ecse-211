                                               Design Report

As a team, you must submit a design report that explains your project requirements, your design process, and
testing procedures. This document provides you with information of what to submit and will be updated
throughout the last weeks of the term to provide you with further information when needed.


Requirements Document (8% of total grade)
During the first week of the project, your focus should be the requirements document. By the end of the first
week, you should have a complete first draft of this document. You may continue to change this document
throughout the project until you submit it.

The requirements document is used to describe the problem statement and the project requirements. You must
write the problem statement of your project based on the client needs. You must also write 60 requirements
for your project split as follows: 20 functions, 20 objectives, and 10 constraints. The problem statement and the
requirements should be based on the client needs document.
