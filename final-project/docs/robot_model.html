Base (both phases)

Two EV3 large motors on B (left) and C (right) drive 2.1 cm wheels 12 cm apart; the code negates both (mounted backwards). Ball caster at the back. BrickPi3 + Pi + battery on top.

Colour sensors on ports 4 (left) and 2 (right), 12 cm apart, 5 cm behind the axle (SENSOR_OFFSET_BY_PHASE), on floor the intake has already swept.

Phase 1: turret

Ultrasonic (port 3) on a medium motor (port A), turret axis 8 cm ahead of the axle, sensor face 2.5 cm from the axis, ±90°. Zero = where it points at power-on: point it straight ahead before starting.

Phase 2: roller v2 + tray

Motor D spins a paper roller with 8 rows of short flaps; the underside sweeps backward, drags pom-poms along a guide 6 mm from the flap tips and releases them up and back into a low tray (one layer of 50 needs ~90 cm²).

Square front, full width: pom-poms can sit 2 cm from a house, i.e. up to 13.2 cm from the lane centre, so the roller has to be ~24 cm wide. A square 25 cm front reaching 11.6 cm can't spin in a street tile (17 cm radius), so Phase 2 drives corners on an arc. Try "At a street corner".

The tray still collides: between the roller guide (4.7 cm ahead of the axle) and the wheels (2.1 cm) there is only ~2 cm.

Phase 2: dump

Motor A swings the tray on an arm. The bucket is level with the table, so pom-poms must leave the tray past the table edge. In the code's drop pose the robot is parallel to the edge with its centre 12.2 cm from it (side dump).

Pick "At the drop-off" and move the arm angle. With a pivot 13 cm up on the edge side, the whole opening clears the edge at about 190°. Over the back (the tray plan's overshot) needs about 250°, and the robot reversed up to the edge. The code's DUMP_TILT_DEG = 110 is a placeholder: measure it on the real arm.
